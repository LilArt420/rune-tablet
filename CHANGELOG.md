# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- The Rune Tablet window: Tablets, Encyclopedia and Disassembly, drawn from the client's runesystem art, opened by the Rune Tablet in Prontera (193, 189).
- The Rune Tablet NPC wears the rune stone look (4_RUNESTONE).
- The window fits its frame (no title overlap, no sideways scrolling), its three tabs answer where they're drawn, the episode arrows (and the episode name) switch between Episodes 17-21, Chapter 1 and the event sets, and it closes when you leave the game.
- Works on the server's own Rune Tablet data and each character's progress; the dialog-menu NPC at (140, 190) is hidden while the mod is on.
