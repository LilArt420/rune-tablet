# Rune Tablet

kRO's Rune Tablet, in its official window, for Ragnarok Offline (Renewal, 1.6.6+).

Talk to the **Rune Tablet** in Prontera (193, 189) and the window opens, drawn
from the client's own Rune Tablet art:

- **Tablets**: every rune set of Episodes 17-21 and Chapter 1. See which of its
  runes you have, what its bonuses do at each count, and upgrade it from +1 to
  +15 at the official success rates (each failure makes the next try a little
  more likely).
- **Encyclopedia**: every rune, with its picture and the materials it takes.
  Activate it once you carry them all.
- **Disassembly**: the items you carry that break down into rune materials, one
  at a time or 30 at once.

It drives the server's own Rune Tablet (added in 1.6.6), so runes and sets you
registered with its NPC carry over, and set bonuses work the same. The old
dialog-menu NPC at Prontera (140, 190) is hidden while this mod is on.

Needs the runesystem art from a kRO fallback GRF for the newest runes.

`client/index.js` is generated by `tools/build.py` from the client's Rune tables
and `tools/plugin.js`.
